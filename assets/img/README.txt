AK CONSTRUCTIONS — landing page images
======================================

Drop your own photos in THIS folder with these exact names and the landing
hero will use them automatically. Until you add them, tasteful built-in
illustrations show instead (nothing looks broken).

1) house1.png  — the framed house photo in the centre of the hero.
   • This is the file the hero currently uses (a full render/photo works great;
     it is shown as a rounded framed image between the words DREAM · SPACES).
   • Recommended size: ~1400 x 1000 px (landscape). A transparent PNG cut-out
     also works. To use a different file, change the <img class="house-photo">
     src in landing.html.

2) city.jpg    — the blurred city / skyline background behind the glass card.
   • A wide landscape photo of buildings or your city (Hassan / Bengaluru).
   • Recommended size: ~2400 x 1400 px, JPG. It is auto-blurred + tinted.

(Optional) You can also swap the small review avatars — see .hero-review in
landing.html; by default they are coloured initials.

Files are referenced from landing.html as  assets/img/house.png  and
assets/img/city.jpg . If a file is missing it is simply ignored.
